# Title

Sikurepi: Cook More, Waste Less | AI Cooking App

# Description

Cook more. Waste less. Make everyday home cooking something to look forward to.

Meet Sikurepi, an AI cooking companion designed to help you use the ingredients you have, discover what to cook, and build a home-cooking habit.

In this demo:
- Create individual dishes or complete set meals with AI.
- Plan your week and add missing ingredients to your shopping list.
- Manage your pantry and scan receipts to add ingredients.
- Follow cooking steps and ask the AI chef for help.
- Personalize suggestions with preferences, allergies, dietary restrictions, and religious considerations.
- Save favorites, discover community recipes, and collect ingredients as you cook.
- Grow through 10 chef ranks, one meal at a time.

Our hope is to help people around the world waste less food and build healthier everyday lives through home cooking.

AI-generated suggestions may contain mistakes. Always check ingredient labels and preparation requirements. Sikurepi does not guarantee allergen-free preparation or religious certification.
